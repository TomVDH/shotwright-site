Logo

The wordmark is cut from a slab of ink. The i is the playhead: a tape-yellow bar that runs behind the letters. Its tip is cut out of the slab as negative space. The slab takes the ink colour of the page; the crop of the i is the favicon.

Motion

The bar slides in behind the letters, showing only through their cut-outs, and eases into the i. 50 frames at 24 fps.

HoldFrames 0 to 12, the bar waits off the left edge.
SlideFrames 12 to 50, behind the letters, cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1).
LandIt decelerates into the i at 0.65 of the width. No overshoot.
