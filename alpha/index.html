Shotwright is a previz tool for film and AI video. Block a scene, place cameras, cut coverage, then export a brief for Seedance or Higgsfield.

alpha-1. Used daily at ZenaStudios.

From a scene to a brief

IBlock it from above.

Place actors, walls, set pieces, lights and cameras. Camera cones and the 180° line show on the plan. G moves, R rotates, as in Blender.

IICut the coverage.

Lay cuts on an edit track, one camera per cut. A cheat moves something for one cut only. Shotwright flags cuts that cross the 180° line.

    IIICompose your packet, for humans or bots.

    One export folder: stills and the animatic for the crew, the prompt and the scene data for the model. Zip and watermark are optional.

    For humans
    • sequence.mp4The animatic, 40 s
    • cut01.png to cut10.pngA greybox still per cut
    • cut05_end.pngThe last frame of a moving shot
    • cut05.mp4The move itself
    For bots
    • prompt.txtThe brief, shot by shot
    • scene.jsonThe scene: cast, set, lights, cameras, cuts

    IVBrief the model.

    The prompt describes the cast, set, lens and move of each shot. Below is a real export of the diner scene. Hover a still to see its depth pass.
